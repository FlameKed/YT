youtube-shorts-bot

youtube-shorts-bot is a private automation tool used by its owner to produce short educational videos and publish them to the owner's own YouTube channels (MoodyVB, Mind Eleven and Zoolanderia).

The tool signs in with Google only to upload videos to these channels and to read their public view statistics. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
